# Mermaid Game — Collectibles, Biomes & Outfits

Design list matched **1:1 to Cornerpond's scope** (see `Cornerpond-Scope.md`): 7 biomes, **161 unique collectibles** (26 per standard biome × 6 + 5 secret), 7 quality ranks, 5 rarity tiers, shiny variants, and a full outfit wardrobe. Styled to the reference boards: mundane river pebbles at the start, ending in aura-quartz / opal / iridescent celestial treasure; kawaii bottled-trinket charm throughout.

**Rarity layout per biome (matches Cornerpond exactly): 9 Common · 8 Rare · 7 Epic · 2 Legendary = 26.**
Global totals: 54 C / 48 R / 42 E / 12 L / 5 Secret = 161.

Every item can also be found **Gleaming** (shiny equivalent — painted with an iridescent sheen pass) and rolls a quality rank **E–S+** (7 ranks, value doubling: 5 / 10 / 20 / 40 / 80 / 160 / 320).
